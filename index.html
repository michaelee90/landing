Hi there!

Mike is a people-focused designer committed to transforming socio-economic and operational data into strategic capability frameworks, scalable adult learning initiatives, and high-impact stakeholder partnerships. With a Bachelor of Fine Arts (Graphic Design) from the Rhode Island School of Design, he enjoys working at the intersection of creativity and structural rigour to resolve complex communication challenges. As an active freelance designer and commission artist, he welcomes collaborations with organisations and clients demanding visual sophistication and detailed nuance for their projects.
